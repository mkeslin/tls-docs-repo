# Other roles

> **Not yet approved for demos.** This page describes the current Court V2 build, which is newer than the version approved for demos. Use it for training and planning. Check [Demoable now](../demoable-now.md) before showing these screens to a customer.

Not everyone at a Court V2 court works the queues. This page covers court staff who read cases, run reports or post payments.

## Read-only user

**Who:** staff who need to look up cases but don't change them, such as front-desk staff or other departments.

**What they can do:**

- Search for and open cases.
- See the **Dashboard**.
- See the **Judge Queue** read-only. The queue shows: *Approve, Needs info and Reject are judge rulings. Your account does not have the judge (Adjudicate) permission.*

**What they can't do:** run outcomes, work the Clerk Queue, take payments, request dismissals, or rule on requests. The Clerk Queue and Prosecutor Queue don't appear for them.

**Practice:** find a case by number, open it, and read its status, plea and next setting from the header.

## Court supervisor

**Who:** court administrators and managers who oversee the court's work.

**What they can do:** run court reports, and see analytics and audits. See [Court reports](../../reports.md).

**What they can't do with this role alone:** open or change cases, or work any queue. A supervisor who also handles cases needs the clerk role as well.

**Practice:** run one court report for last month, and find an audit entry.

## Payments user

**Who:** cashiers or finance staff who post payments but don't do clerk work.

**Setup:** usually the accounting role plus the read-only court role. See [Role setup](appendix-role-setup.md).

**What they can do:** look up cases and post payments. See [Payments](../../payments.md).

**Things to know on a Court V2 court:**

- A pay in full request at the counter is taken by the clerk as part of the request. The money is held until the judge approves. If the judge rejects the request, or it's revoked, the held money is refunded by check.
- A compliance dismissal fee is collected before the judge rules. If the request is denied or cancelled, the fee stays posted and is flagged for the court administrator. There's no automatic refund.

**Practice:** post a training payment and explain the difference between a pending payment and an accepted one.
