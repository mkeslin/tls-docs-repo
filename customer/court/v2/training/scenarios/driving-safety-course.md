# Scenario: Driving safety course

> **Not yet approved for demos.** This page describes the current Court V2 build, which is newer than the version approved for demos. Use it for training and planning. Check [Demoable now](../../demoable-now.md) before showing these screens to a customer.

## When to use

The violator asks to take a driving safety course (DSC) to have a traffic case dismissed.

## Who

- **Clerk** runs the request and submits it.
- **Violator** answers and signs on the tablet or paper, or requests it online.
- **Judge** grants it, and rules on any request for more time.

## Check eligibility first

The driving safety course card isn't available for a commercial driver license, workers present, leaving the scene, a school bus offense, 95 mph or more or 25 mph or more over the limit (CCP 45A.352(a)(5), no override), or a missing offense type. See [Court programs and eligibility](../../court-programs-eligibility.md).

## At the counter (tablet)

1. Open the case and select **Driving safety course** in **Diversions**. The outcome shows four steps: **Plea and jury waiver**, **Violator answers on the tablet**, **Clerk review and submit**, and **Judge review**.

   ![Driving safety course request](../../images/court-v2-dsc-outcome-open.png)

   *The violator's tablet answers fill in while the clerk completes the rest.*

2. Pair the tablet. The violator answers the plea, jury waiver and **military status**, then signs.
3. Fill in the clerk fields: **License state** (only when the person record has none), **Last DSC completed**, **Proof of insurance** (required) and the **Driving record** (CCP 45A.359).
4. Until the military status is answered, the screen shows *Answer the military status question before submitting*.
5. Select **Submit for judge review**. Submitting sends the request to the judge; it doesn't enroll the course.

## On paper

1. Switch on **Paper (print and hand-sign)** and print the forms.
2. Have the violator hand-sign. Enter the military status from the signed form.
3. Check **Proof verified by clerk** instead of uploading proof of insurance, and submit for the judge.

## Online

The violator can request a driving safety course on the portal, answer the military status question, and add the documents. The portal shows the **90-day** deadline read-only and explains the judge must approve before the course starts.

## Judge

The judge reviews the request in the Judge Queue and selects **Approve**, **Needs Info** or **Reject**. Eligibility is checked again on approval.

## After approval

The course is due **90 days** after the judge approves.

### More time during the course

1. Open **Case requests and orders** and use the **DSC more time** panel.
2. Send the request. The judge sets the new due date (CCP 45A.355). If the judge rules on paper, record the paper ruling.

Online, the portal shows: *Need more time to finish the driving safety course? You can ask the judge for more time.*

### After a failure to complete

1. Use the **DSC after failure** panel to send the request to the judge, or to record the judge's paper ruling.
2. This is allowed until guilt is adjudicated. Only the judge rules on it.

## Can't or blocked

- The clerk can't grant the course or rule on more time.
- Driving safety course plans never apply to Youth Diversion.
- Only one request can be open on the case.
