# Scenario: Portal requests

> **Not yet approved for demos.** This page describes the current Court V2 build, which is newer than the version approved for demos. Use it for training and planning. Check [Demoable now](../../demoable-now.md) before showing these screens to a customer.

## When to use

A violator uses the online portal to look up a citation, pay, or ask for an outcome, and the clerk or judge needs to act on what they sent.

## Who

- **Violator** uses the portal.
- **Clerk** reviews portal requests and contact updates in the Clerk Queue.
- **Judge** rules on program and relief requests sent from the portal.

## What the violator does

1. Looks up the citation.

   ![Citation lookup](../../images/court-v2-portal-lookup.png)

   *The violator looks up a citation.*

2. Confirms their identity with a one-time code. The code isn't needed just to pay or to look at the options.
3. Opens the citation dashboard, which shows the balance and links to pay or resolve the case.
4. On the resolve page, picks an outcome card. Cards depend on the case, for example pay in full, a not guilty plea, a driving safety course, deferred disposition, a payment plan, community service, hardship or a compliance dismissal.

   ![Resolve options](../../images/court-v2-portal-resolve-options.png)

   *The outcome cards offered online for this case.*

5. Each card shows **Start or resume**, or **Complete at counter** when it isn't available online.

## Where portal requests go

| Request | Goes to |
|---------|---------|
| Not guilty plea | **Clerk Queue > Violator portal**. Entered only when the clerk approves it. |
| Contact updates | **Clerk Queue > Violator portal** |
| Programs and relief (driving safety course, deferred, payment plan, community service, hardship, extension) | Judge Queue |

## Clerk review

1. Open **Clerk Queue > Violator portal**. The list is titled **Portal requests and contact updates** and shows each request's **Source**.
2. Select **Review case and documents** to check the case.
3. Select **Review request** and choose **Approve** or **Needs info**. **Deny** appears only for compliance dismissals that can be declined.

See [Violator portal requests](../clerk-guide.md#violator-portal-requests).

## What isn't available online

| Case | What the violator sees |
|------|------------------------|
| Family-violence offense | Pay in full and pleas show **Complete at counter**. The portal explains that an in-person appearance is required (CCP 45A.151(d)). |
| Minor who needs a parent present | Online pleas and payments aren't available. |
| Youth Diversion | *Juvenile combined is not available online. Complete at counter.* |
| Guilty or no contest on its own | Not offered. Those pleas are part of other outcomes. |

## Notices

The violator can read an FTA or CPF show cause notice, open the documents, and acknowledge receipt. See [FTA and failure to pay](fta-cpf.md).
