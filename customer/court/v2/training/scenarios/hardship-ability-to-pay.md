# Scenario: Hardship and ability to pay

> **Not yet approved for demos.** This page describes the current Court V2 build, which is newer than the version approved for demos. Use it for training and planning. Check [Demoable now](../../demoable-now.md) before showing these screens to a customer.

## When to use

The violator says they can't afford to pay the fine and costs, or asks the judge for relief.

## Who

- **Clerk** runs the hardship request at the counter or on paper.
- **Violator** fills in the hardship form on the tablet, on paper or online.
- **Judge** rules on the request and handles the ability-to-pay inquiry and waivers.

## The hardship form

Hardship is its own request using the full TMCEC indigent application. The financial disclosure fields are required to submit.

The form has **Community service** and **Extend to a later date** checkboxes. They only tell the judge what the violator is asking for. They never create a community service order or an extension, and hardship is never a combined request.

## At the counter (tablet)

1. Open the case and select **Hardship** in **Financial relief**.
2. Pair the tablet. The violator completes the financial disclosure, adds at least one piece of evidence, and signs.
3. Review the answers and select **Submit for judge review**.

## On paper

The violator signs a sworn financial form, and Thin Line tracks the answers. **Not yet approved for demos:** paper hardship is still being aligned with the evidence step in the current build.

## Online

The violator opens the hardship card on the portal, completes the form and adds at least one piece of evidence to submit.

![Hardship on the portal](../../images/court-v2-portal-hardship.png)

*The hardship form on the portal.*

## Judge

The judge reviews the request in the Judge Queue and selects **Approve**, **Needs Info** or **Reject**.

Judge-only actions on the case:

- **Ability-to-pay inquiry.**
- **Fine or cost waiver** after a judgment.
- **Commitment terms.**

## Relief without a hardship request

Community service and payment plans can be requested on their own. When the judge approves one, that approval is the ability-to-pay finding. See [Community service](community-service.md) and [Plea and pay](plea-and-pay.md).

## Known gaps

These combinations aren't shipped. Each is "Known gap, not shipped":

- Extension and community service together
- Hardship and community service together
- Hardship and pay in full together
- Extension and hardship together

See [Demoable now](../../demoable-now.md#known-gaps-not-shipped).

## Can't or blocked

- The clerk can't rule on hardship, waive fines or costs, or hold the ability-to-pay inquiry.
- Only one request can be open on the case.
