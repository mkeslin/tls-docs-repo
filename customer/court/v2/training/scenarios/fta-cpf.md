# Scenario: FTA and failure to pay

> **Not yet approved for demos.** This page describes the current Court V2 build, which is newer than the version approved for demos. Use it for training and planning. Check [Demoable now](../../demoable-now.md) before showing these screens to a customer.

## When to use

The violator failed to appear (FTA) or failed to pay (CPF), and the court is working the notice, show cause or warrant steps.

## What's the same on a Court V2 court

FTA and CPF work the same as on any court. Use the existing work queues and screens:

- **Work queues:** FTA initial appearance, FTA show cause, Surety bond show cause, Missed payment and Collections eligible. See [Work queues](../../../work-queues.md).
- **How-to:** [Handle an FTA and court warrant](../../../how-tos/handle-fta-and-court-warrant.md).
- **Reference:** [FTA, warrants and bonds](../../../fta-warrants-bonds.md).

## Who does what

| Step | Who |
|------|-----|
| FTA notice | Clerk |
| CPF show cause | Clerk |
| Commitment discharge | Clerk records it |
| FTA warrant one-step | Judge only |
| CPF warrant one-step | Judge only |
| CPF ROH | Judge only |
| Commitment terms | Judge only |

## Online

When the violator has an FTA notice or a CPF show cause notice, they can read it on the portal, open any attached documents, and acknowledge receipt. The acknowledgment is time-stamped. There's no upload, and acknowledging doesn't clear or issue a warrant.

## Resolving the case afterwards

Once the violator comes in, the case workspace offers the outcomes the case allows, such as pay in full, a payment plan or community service. Each still goes to the judge. See [Plea and pay](plea-and-pay.md).

## Can't or blocked

- Clerks can't issue a warrant or set commitment terms.
