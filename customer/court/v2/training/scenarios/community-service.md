# Scenario: Community service

> **Not yet approved for demos.** This page describes the current Court V2 build, which is newer than the version approved for demos. Use it for training and planning. Check [Demoable now](../../demoable-now.md) before showing these screens to a customer.

## When to use

The violator asks to discharge the fine and costs by doing community service.

## Who

- **Clerk** runs the request at the counter.
- **Violator** proposes hours or an amount and signs.
- **Judge** approves and can edit the terms.

## At the counter (tablet)

1. Open the case and select **Community service** in **Financial relief**.

   ![Community service request](../../images/court-v2-community-service-open.png)

   *A community service request, with the terms the judge can edit.*

2. The screen explains the terms: *Each 8 hours discharges not less than $150. No more than 16 hours per week unless the court finds more would not work a hardship. The judge may edit these terms.* The legal basis shown is CCP 45A.252, 45A.254 and 45A.257.
3. The violator proposes the hours or amount and signs on the tablet.
4. Select **Submit for judge review**.

No evidence is required, and there's no community service fee.

## Online

Community service can appear among the outcome cards on the portal, depending on the case. On the hardship form, ticking **Community service** only tells the judge what the violator is asking for. It never creates a community service order.

## Judge

The judge selects **Approve**, **Needs Info** or **Reject**. Community service order terms are judge-only, and the judge can edit the proposed terms.

## After approval

- Community service starts only when the judge approves it. Submitting, rejecting or returning it with Needs Info never starts it.
- The judge's approval is the ability-to-pay finding.
- If there's no judgment on the case yet, approving community service enters a guilty judgment.

## Can't or blocked

- Clerks can't approve community service or set its terms.
- Community service combined with an extension or a hardship request is "Known gap, not shipped".
- Child-specific community service options aren't supported yet. See [Compliance gaps](../../compliance-gaps.md).
