# Scenario: Deferred disposition

> **Not yet approved for demos.** This page describes the current Court V2 build, which is newer than the version approved for demos. Use it for training and planning. Check [Demoable now](../../demoable-now.md) before showing these screens to a customer.

## When to use

The violator asks for deferred disposition: the judgment is deferred while they meet the conditions, and the case can be dismissed afterwards.

## Who

- **Clerk** runs the request and submits it.
- **Violator** answers and signs on the tablet, on paper, or requests it online.
- **Judge** grants or denies it. Only a judge can grant a court program (CCP 45A.307).

## Check eligibility first

Thin Line checks eligibility when the card is shown, when you submit, and again when the judge approves. The card shows the reason when deferred isn't available. Common reasons:

| Situation | Result |
|-----------|--------|
| Commercial driver license on a moving violation | Not available |
| Workers present | Not available |
| Date of birth missing on a moving violation | Blocked with *Date of birth required for Deferred on a moving violation* |
| Under 25 on a moving violation | Allowed; a driving safety course is added as a condition of the same plan |
| Leaving the scene, school bus offense, or 95+ mph / 25+ over | Deferred may still be offered, at the judge's discretion |

See [Court programs and eligibility](../../court-programs-eligibility.md) for the full list.

## At the counter (tablet)

1. Open the case and select **Deferred disposition** in **Diversions**.
2. Pair the tablet. The violator reviews the documents and answers the plea, jury waiver and other questions, then signs.
3. Review the answers and fill in the clerk fields.
4. Select **Submit for judge review**. The screen reminds you that submitting sends the request to the judge and doesn't enroll the program.

## On paper

1. Switch on **Paper (print and hand-sign)**.
2. Print the forms and have the violator hand-sign them.
3. Mark them hand-signed, tick any paper checks, and submit for the judge.

## Online

The portal shows the deferral period read-only, **180 days**, and explains that the judge must approve the request before it starts. Submitting online doesn't enroll the violator.

## Judge

The request appears in the Judge Queue. It can show a **Statutory notes (informational)** box with the line *Approve re-checks these rules against current case facts.* The judge selects **Approve**, **Needs Info** or **Reject**.

## After approval

- Deferred disposition is granted with a **180-day** period. A longer period can't be set.
- The existing deferred work queues apply: Deferred missed deadline, Deferred FTC show cause, and Deferred ready to close. See [Court programs](../../../court-programs.md).

## Can't or blocked

- Clerks can't grant deferred disposition. The screen says: *Only a judge can grant a court program (CCP 45A.307). Submit it to the judge.*
- Only one request can be open on the case.
- Youth Diversion and juvenile deferred disposition are separate outcomes. See [Youth and juvenile cases](youth-juvenile.md).
