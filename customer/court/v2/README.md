# Court V2

Court V2 is the outcome-based way of working court cases. The violator (or a clerk helping them) picks an outcome, such as pay in full, a driving safety course or a payment plan. Thin Line puts together the forms and steps that outcome needs, and the request goes to the right queue for a decision. Only the judge approves, rejects or returns a request, and the judge signs every judgment and dismissal.

## Turning on Court V2

Court V2 is turned on per court. An administrator opens **Admin > Agency > General** and turns on **Turn on Court V2 for this agency**. Only users with agency administration rights can change it. The switch is off by default, and once it's on, it stays on.

The one switch turns on everything in Court V2: the online portal, the in-person clerk workspace, paper outcomes, the Clerk, Prosecutor and Judge Queues, the dashboard card, and the Court V2 case panels.

For a Court V2 court, the V2 workspace, queues and portal are the daily workflow. The work queues, calendar and reports work the same with or without Court V2, so the [Court](../README.md) pages for those areas still apply. Courts that haven't turned on Court V2 keep using the [Court](../README.md) pages for everything.

![Court V2 online portal lookup](images/court-v2-portal-lookup.png)

*Violators start at the online portal by looking up their citation. Clerks start from the case workspace.*
