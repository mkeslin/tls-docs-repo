# Custody episode charges and property

After **Accept into Custody**, a **custody episode** ties together bookings, charges, property, and other intake work. This page covers episode-level **Charges** and property patterns that extend booking intake.

## Open a custody episode

Use **Jail Intake** → **Booking Search** (or custody episode search when your agency uses it) to open the episode. Episode tabs include **Charges** and property views linked to the active booking.

## Charges tab

The **Charges** tab lists all charges on the episode in one table (source booking, type, statute, description, level).

| Action | When it appears |
|--------|-----------------|
| **Add Charge / Hold** | When you may correct charges and the related booking is in an **accepted** status |
| **Add Offense** | Same conditions — opens offense search to add a statutory offense |
| **Correct** | Per-row correction for an existing charge |

Toolbar actions use the same permission and booking-status rules: add and correct are not offered until the booking is accepted and your role allows episode edits.

### Booking step vs episode tab

On the booking **Charges** step during intake, **Add Charge / Hold** is the primary button; open the split menu (**More add options**) for **Add Offense**.

## Property bags and seal status

On the **Property** step (booking or episode property viewer):

| Task | Behavior |
|------|----------|
| **Add Container** | Create a property bag and add items |
| **Seal** | Unsealed bags show a seal action; optional seal number in the dialog |
| **Open** | Sealed bags show an open action to make the bag editable again |
| Seal status | Icons reflect sealed vs unsealed; you cannot edit bag contents while sealed |

Clearing optional fields (seal number, storage location, item details, and similar) saves as empty and removes the stored value.

Currency fields such as **Bond Amount**, **Cash amount**, and **Estimated value** accept two decimal places.

## Reports and refresh

Charge and property toolbars include refresh controls and step reports where your agency prints intake PDFs. Long steps keep the step toolbar visible while you scroll.
