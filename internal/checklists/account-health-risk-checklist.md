## Red flags (any one ⇒ at least Watch; two+ or any Critical ⇒ At-risk)

### Support / product

| Severity | Criteria |
|----------|----------|
| Critical | Open **P1** or suspected security / CJI exposure |
| High | ≥ **3** P2+ tickets in last 30 days, or same issue reopened twice |
| Medium | Recurring how-to noise suggesting training gap |

### Relationship / adoption

| Severity | Criteria |
|----------|----------|
| Critical | Champion left with **no** replacement named |
| High | Sold module **not** in routine use after training window |
| High | Explicit dissatisfaction / escalation to founder |
| Medium | Key role (dispatch, clerk, admin) not trained or not logging in |

### Commercial / billing

| Severity | Criteria |
|----------|----------|
| Critical | Non-payment / collections path active |
| High | Contract dispute or cancellation language |
| Medium | Unexpected invoice friction delaying trust |

### Qualitative / other

| Severity | Criteria |
|----------|----------|
| High | “We might switch” or competitive evaluation underway |
| Medium | Long silence (no reply to last two outreach attempts) |

---

## Band (pick one)

| Band | Rule of thumb | Cadence |
|------|---------------|---------|
| **Healthy** | No Critical/High; at most one Medium | Quarterly |
| **Watch** | One High **or** multiple Medium; no Critical | Quarterly (pull forward if worsens) |
| **At-risk** | Any Critical **or** two+ High | Monthly minimum; next action ≤ 14 days |

When unsure between Watch and At-risk, choose **At-risk**.
