## Must-pass (all required)

- [ ] Health band is **Healthy** (or documented rare exception)
- [ ] Live / post-hypercare (not mid-implementation scramble)
- [ ] Named champion who can speak for the agency
- [ ] No open P1 or active cancellation / collections fight
- [ ] Thin Line would be proud for a peer to call them

---

## Strong signals (prefer 2+)

- [ ] Clear operational win they can name in plain language
- [ ] Using sold modules in routine daily/weekly work
- [ ] Champion is responsive and positive in recent touches
- [ ] Peer-relevant size / region / module mix for our ICP
- [ ] Already offered intros or praise unprompted

---

## Caution / defer (any one ⇒ wait or skip)

- [ ] Watch band without a clear recovery path
- [ ] Major training gap still blocking adoption
- [ ] Champion turnover with no replacement
- [ ] Recent public or political sensitivity (local news, leadership change)

---

## Decision

| Decision | Rule of thumb |
|----------|----------------|
| ★ Ask now | Must-pass all + enough strong signals |
| ★ Wait | Caution flags, or success too new (< ~30 days post-handoff unless they volunteer) |
| ★ Do not ask | Failed must-pass |
