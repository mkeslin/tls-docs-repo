## Within one business day

- [ ] Notes / cards / photos gathered in one place
- [ ] Each promising contact triaged: ★ Lead · ★ Customer · ★ Partner · ★ Skip

---

## Leads

- [ ] Pipedrive org/person created or updated
- [ ] Origin set (Event / Conference / per GTM fields)
- [ ] Next activity ≤ **7 days**
- [ ] Handed to Acquire rhythm if sales follow-up needed

---

## Customers

- [ ] Touch logged on customer org
- [ ] Intro offers → [Capture referral](../sops/advocate/capture-referral.md)
- [ ] Reference offers → [Request customer reference](../sops/advocate/request-customer-reference.md)

---

## Community / speaking

- [ ] Speaking or peer-group opportunities noted (who, topic, timing)
- [ ] Owner + next step dated

---

## Product feedback

- [ ] Short feedback list sent to Product (no ship-date promises)
- [ ] Urgent pain routed via Support triage if live-customer issue

---

## Hub

- [ ] Advocate two-week plan updated if this event was a period priority
