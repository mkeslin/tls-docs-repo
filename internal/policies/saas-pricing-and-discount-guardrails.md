**Document type:** Policy  
**Status:** v2.0 — <mark style="color:red;">Draft</mark> (working commercial guidance)  
**Audience:** Internal — Acquire, founders (do **not** publish to customers)  
**Related:** [Acquire authority matrix](acquire-authority.md) · [Prepare proposal](../sops/acquire/prepare-proposal.md) · [Migration Pricing Policy](migration-pricing.md) · [Go-to-market](../strategy/go-to-market.md)

---

## Purpose

Give Acquire a **pricing card** so standard proposals can go out without a fire drill, while protecting margin and scope.

This policy covers **recurring SaaS** and common one-time fees. Detailed migration tiers also live in [Migration Pricing Policy](migration-pricing.md) — keep the two consistent.

**Positioning:** Thin Line is a **premium small-agency** option — fully featured product, easier to approve than enterprise vendors, priced to signal quality and fund excellent support. Aim roughly **15%–30% above** earlier internal “book of business” levels in the strongest segments. Be willing to discount **strategically**, but **do not start at the discounted price**.

**Do not reduce the standard price card** to win market share. Use the controlled [Market-share growth program](#market-share-growth-program) instead. Launch and quote these **list prices**; during the market-share phase, expect average **realized** recurring SaaS at about **85%–90% of list** — not a rewritten card.

**Do not use “typical annual SaaS” as a single number.** Use **list price / normal close range / strategic floor**.

---

## Pricing principle

Select the applicable **list price** based on agency size and product scope. **Adjust upward** for operational complexity, integrations, transaction volume, multi-agency use, or enhanced support requirements.

Acquire should **quote at list price** as the standard proposal price and may negotiate within the **normal close range**. The **strategic floor** is **not published** and **not routinely offered**. Prices below the strategic floor, free implementation or migration, nonstandard contract terms, and total economic concessions above applicable discount / term authority require **founder / deal desk approval**.

All concessions must be **shown and recorded explicitly**. Do **not** hide discounts by reducing implementation, migration, integration, support, or hardware charges without documenting the full **undiscounted** value.

## How to read price fields

| Field | Meaning | Who may use |
|-------|---------|-------------|
| **List price** | Standard proposal / asking price | Default first quote — always start here |
| **Normal close range** | Where most negotiated deals should land | Acquire may negotiate from list into this range without approval |
| **Strategic floor** | Lowest Acquire may go alone; **not published**; **not routinely offered** | At or above = Acquire OK with documented reason; **below** = founder / deal desk |

Prefer quoting at **list price**, closing inside the **normal close range**, and touching the **strategic floor** only when necessary and documented.

### RMS-only and CAD-only (core modules)

Eliminate broad “half price” or 55–70% shortcuts. Each core module still carries platform, support, security, implementation, and account-management cost.

Compute single-module **list price** as a percentage of the officer band’s **RMS + CAD list price**, then apply the same list → normal close → strategic-floor discipline (negotiate down from that single-module list; do not start discounted).

| Package | Percentage of RMS + CAD **list** | Notes |
|---------|--------------------------------:|--------|
| **RMS only** | **70%** | Fixed |
| **CAD only** | **70%–75%** | Use the **higher** end when dispatch is complex; CAD often carries greater real-time ops/support expectations |
| **RMS + CAD** | **100%** | Full stack row — do **not** add RMS-only + CAD-only |
| **Second module added later** | Upgrade to **current combined-package** price | See below |

**Ordinary minima (Acquire):** even after %-of-list math, do not quote below:

| Package | Ordinary minimum (Acquire) | Founder-approved strategic minimum |
|---------|---------------------------:|-----------------------------------:|
| **RMS only** | **$6,000** | **$5,000** |
| **CAD only** | **$7,500** | **$5,000** |
| **RMS + CAD** | Band **strategic floor** | Below band floor = founder |

Also respect the absolute **$5,000** company floor — only with founder approval for single-module deals.

Example (8–12 officers, RMS only): full-stack list $19,000 × **70%** = **$13,300** list → negotiate within a normal close derived from that list; ordinary minimum **$6,000**.

### Adding the second core module later

Protect against buying one module cheaply and adding the second soon after:

| Situation | Rule |
|-----------|------|
| Customer has **RMS only** or **CAD only**, then adds the other core module | **Upgrade to the then-current combined RMS + CAD package** price for their officer band (list / normal close / strategic floor at time of expansion) |
| Bundle / multi-year concession on the combined package | Only with **approved** discount — still at or above the full-stack **strategic floor** unless founder approves lower |
| Partial “add the delta only” forever | **Not allowed** as the default — the combined package price is the source of truth |

Document the upgrade math in Pipedrive on the expansion deal.

**Acquire authority (LE):** final annual SaaS at or above the applicable **ordinary minimum** / band **strategic floor** → Acquire alone. Below ordinary minimum down to **$5,000**, or below band strategic floor on full stack, or 41+ → founder / deal desk.

## Court SaaS price bands

Court is sold on an **RMS/CAD path** per [Go-to-market](../strategy/go-to-market.md) (existing Thin Line customer or new combined deal). ICP: **one clerk / one judge (1×1)**.

**Sizing metric:** average **citations per month**. If the court only knows annual filings, divide by 12. Note the assumption in Pipedrive. Adjust **upward** from list for integrations or complexity per [Pricing principle](#pricing-principle).

| Profile | Average citations / month | List price | Normal close range | Authority |
|--------|--------------------------:|-----------:|-------------------:|-----------|
| **Light** | Under 50 | $7,500 | $6,000–$7,500 | Acquire |
| **Typical** | 50–200 | $11,000 | $9,000–$11,000 | Acquire |
| **Busy 1×1** | 201–400 | $15,000 | $12,000–$15,000 | Acquire |
| Custom | Above 400, or multi-clerk / multi-judge / multi-jurisdiction | Custom | Founder review | Founder / deal desk |

| Floor | Amount | When |
|-------|-------:|------|
| **Ordinary** (Acquire) | Bottom of the applicable **normal close range** for the citation band | Standard 1×1 quotes — start at **list**, not at the bottom |
| **Strategic floor** (founder) | **$5,000** | Extremely light court only — **not** the ordinary under-50 quote |

Do **not** use **$5,000** as the default light-volume price. Under-50 ordinary list is **$7,500**; **$5,000** requires founder approval.

**Court + LE together:** quote Court from this section **plus** LE from the officer bands (do not absorb Court into the LE total silently).

## Jail SaaS price bands

Jail is sold on an **RMS/CAD path** per [Go-to-market](../strategy/go-to-market.md). Current ICP: about **~15 beds** proven; larger jails need more product/test capacity — treat **41+** as founder review.

Jail-management pricing in the market is largely **quote-based** and varies widely by booking, classification, movements, property, medical, release, state reporting, and integrations. Do **not** assume the absence of published low prices means **$15,000** is aggressive for the ~15-bed ICP.

| Rule | Guidance |
|------|----------|
| **Primary metric** | **Rated bed capacity** — **not** today’s inmate headcount |
| **Why not inmate count** | Headcount swings day to day; beds are stable for quoting |
| **Optional cross-check** | Average daily population (ADP), if known |
| **~15-bed ICP** | Treat **$15,000** as **list** and about **$12,000** as expected close — do **not** start from the 1–10 band bottom ($7,500) |

| Rated beds | List price | Normal close range | Authority |
|----------:|-----------:|-------------------:|-----------|
| 1–10 | $9,000 | $7,500–$9,000 | Acquire |
| 11–20 | $15,000 | $12,000–$15,000 | Acquire (~15-bed ICP) |
| 21–40 | $27,500 | $22,000–$27,500 | Acquire with caution — founder if product-risk or heavy custom |
| 41+ | Custom | Founder review | Founder / deal desk |

Quote at **list price** first; negotiate into the **normal close range**. Bottom of the range is not the default ask.

### Booking activity and complexity (cross-check)

After selecting the bed band, adjust using booking activity and scope:

| Factor | Pricing effect |
|--------|----------------|
| **Low booking turnover** | Standard bed-band price |
| **High turnover relative to bed count** | **+10%–20%** on the annual Jail SaaS |
| **Multiple booking locations** | **+10%–20%** |
| **Complex external interfaces** | Priced **separately** (not absorbed into the bed-band SaaS) |
| **Enhanced 24/7 SLA** | **Premium support** charge (separate line) |

Document bed count, turnover judgment, and any uplift in Pipedrive.

**Jail + LE together:** quote Jail from this section **plus** LE (and Court if any). Do not fold Jail into the LE officer total.

## One-time fees

### Implementation / deployment

One-time implementation fee (deployment). Quote inside the applicable range; **complex** = custom / founder.

| Deployment | One-time implementation fee |
|------------|----------------------------:|
| Single LE module (RMS **or** CAD) | $4,000–$7,500 |
| RMS + CAD | $7,500–$15,000 |
| Court | $4,000–$7,500 |
| Jail | $5,000–$10,000 |
| Multi-product deployment | $12,500–$25,000 |
| Complex deployment | Custom |

For **market-share** deals, Acquire may **include or discount** implementation for selected customers — but the proposal must still **display the full fee** and show the concession as a separate line. Do **not** set implementation to **$0** as the only figure (that establishes a $0 reference price).

**Required display pattern when discounted:**

```text
Implementation:              $10,000
New customer incentive:      ($5,000)
Net implementation:           $5,000
```

That communicates value and keeps the undiscounted fee visible. Record the same math in Pipedrive.

| Authority | Guidance |
|-----------|----------|
| Implementation fee **within** the table range, no discount | Acquire alone |
| Partial discount / “new customer incentive” with full fee still shown | Acquire alone if net is still meaningful and SaaS authority is met; document reason |
| **$0 net** implementation (full waiver) or fee **outside** range / complex | Founder / deal desk |

### Migration

One-time data conversion / migration fee. Prefer completing the [Legacy System Migration Assessment](../assessments/legacy-system-migration-assessment.md) before firming the quote; proposal may say “migration priced after assessment” when not ready. Detail and assessment gate: [Migration Pricing Policy](migration-pricing.md).

| Migration type | One-time fee |
|----------------|-------------:|
| Customer-completed template import | $2,500 |
| Simple structured migration | $5,000–$7,500 |
| Standard legacy-system migration | $10,000–$15,000 |
| Complex records and attachments | $15,000–$30,000 |
| Multiple source systems | Custom |

If migration is discounted for strategic / market-share reasons, use the same **full fee → incentive → net** display pattern as [implementation](#implementation--deployment) — do not quote only $0.

| Authority | Guidance |
|-----------|----------|
| Fee **within** the table after assessment supports the type | Acquire alone |
| Partial discount with full fee still shown | Acquire alone if documented; escalate if net is $0 or economics are heavy |
| **Multiple source systems**, fee **outside** table, or **$0** waiver | Founder / deal desk |

### Training

**Included** with a standard deployment (state the session count on the proposal / order form):

- Standard remote launch training
- Administrator training
- Recorded materials
- A **defined number of sessions** (document the count — do not leave “unlimited”)

**Charge for** extras:

| Additional service | Price |
|--------------------|------:|
| Additional remote session | $1,000 |
| Custom remote training day | $2,500 |
| On-site training day | $3,500 plus travel |
| Annual refresher package | $2,500 |

| Authority | Guidance |
|-----------|----------|
| Included package + priced extras from the table | Acquire alone |
| Waiving / heavily discounting paid training extras | Document explicitly; founder if net is $0 or package is nonstandard |

### Integrations

External interfaces are **not** absorbed into SaaS list price. Quote **setup** (one-time) and **annual maintenance** separately.

| Integration | Setup | Annual maintenance |
|-------------|------:|-------------------:|
| Simple supported interface | $3,500–$5,000 | $1,500–$2,500 |
| Standard third-party integration | $7,500–$12,500 | $2,500–$5,000 |
| Complex / custom integration | $15,000+ | Custom |

| Authority | Guidance |
|-----------|----------|
| Simple or standard within table ranges | Acquire alone — document interface name/scope in Pipedrive |
| Complex / custom, or outside table | Founder / deal desk |
| Discounting setup or maintenance | Show full fee → incentive → net; $0 waiver → founder |

## Discount / approval authority

### Law enforcement

Use **list price → normal close range → strategic floor** (never a single “typical annual SaaS” number).

| Final LE annual vs band | Authority |
|-------------------------|-----------|
| At **list price** or within the **normal close range** (full stack or single-module %-of-list) | Acquire alone |
| Below normal close down to band **strategic floor** / single-module **ordinary minimum** | Acquire alone — document why; floor is **not routinely offered** |
| Below ordinary minimum to **$5,000** (single-module), below band strategic floor (full stack), or **41+** officers | Founder / deal desk |
| Expansion that skips full-stack reset when adding second core module | Founder only (default rule is reset) |
| Free implementation / migration, nonstandard terms, or hidden economic concessions | Founder / deal desk — see [Concessions](#concessions-must-be-explicit) |
| Fee waiver, free modules, or $0 migration | Founder only |

Touching the **strategic floor** is for competition or strategic Priority 1 logos when needed — **not** the starting price and **not** a substitute for term commitment. Longer terms buy **commitment** (see [Contract terms](#contract-terms)).

### Court and Jail

| Guidance | Authority |
|----------|-----------|
| Court at/above bottom of citation-band **normal close range**; quote **list price** first | Acquire alone |
| Court **$5,000** strategic floor (extremely light only) | Founder |
| Jail within bed-band **normal close range** (≤40); quote **list** first; apply booking uplifts | Acquire alone (21–40: caution / founder if risky) |
| Court above 400 / multi-staff (**custom**), Jail **41+**, complex interfaces / 24×7 premium as scoped, or below band bottom without approval | Founder / deal desk |

## Concessions must be explicit

| Rule | Guidance |
|------|----------|
| **Record everything** | Show list / undiscounted value and every concession in the proposal/order form and Pipedrive |
| **No hidden discounts** | Do **not** lower implementation, migration, integration, support, or hardware charges to “make SaaS look higher” (or the reverse) without documenting full undiscounted value |
| **Implementation concessions** | Always show **full fee → incentive line → net** ([Implementation / deployment](#implementation--deployment)); never quote only $0 |
| **Total economics** | If the package of concessions exceeds term-discount / pricing authority, escalate even if SaaS alone is above strategic floor |
| **Approval triggers** | Below strategic floor; free implementation or migration; nonstandard contract terms; escalator waived/frozen; automatic stacking of multi-year + bundle discounts; special termination with full discount retained |

## Market-share growth program

**Do not cut the list price card** to chase logos. Win price-sensitive and strategic deals through this **controlled** program so the market still sees a premium product.

Quote **list** first. Program incentives are named concessions (full fee → incentive → net). Expect average realized recurring SaaS around **85%–90% of list** during the market-share phase — that is an outcome, not a new public price.

For qualifying agencies, offer **one or more** of the following (document which program(s) in Pipedrive). Do **not** auto-stack every lever with term/bundle discounts without founder approval ([Contract terms](#contract-terms)).

### Founding-agency incentive

| Element | Guidance |
|---------|----------|
| **Year-one SaaS** | **10%–15% off** Year 1 only |
| **Thereafter** | **Standard** list / normal close and **5%** escalator (or fixed year-by-year schedule) |
| **Term** | **Three-year** agreement |
| **In exchange** | Reference call or case study; logo usage; feedback participation |
| **Authority** | Acquire within 10–15% Year-1; deeper or multi-year SaaS cuts → founder |

### Competitive replacement credit

| Element | Guidance |
|---------|----------|
| **What** | Discount **implementation** and/or **migration** (show full fee → credit → net) |
| **Recurring SaaS** | **Preserve** standard SaaS pricing (list / normal close) — do not permanently lower the card |
| **Proof** | Require evidence of an **existing competing contract** |
| **Expiry** | Incentive expires after a **defined date** (state on proposal) |
| **Authority** | Acquire for implementation/migration credits within one-time fee tables; SaaS cuts or $0 net one-times → founder |

## Contract terms

Standardize these commercial terms on every deal. Longer terms buy **commitment**, not simply a lower price. Do **not** leave renewal pricing silent on the order form / proposal.

| Term | Standard |
|------|----------|
| **Default initial term** | **Three years** |
| **Billing** | **Annual billing in advance** |
| **Standard annual increase** | **5%** |
| **One-year term** | **Full list price** — no term discount |
| **Three-year price commitment** | Maximum **5%** discount off the otherwise applicable price (still respect **strategic floor**) |
| **Five-year non-cancellable commitment** | Maximum **8%** discount (same strategic-floor rules) |
| **Multi-year + bundle discounts** | Do **not** automatically **stack** — choose the better of the allowed concessions, or get founder approval for any combined package |
| **Special termination rights** | **Reduce or eliminate** term / bundle discounts (convenience outs, short notice, appropriation-only outs beyond standard, etc.) |

### Annual increase (escalator)

| Rule | Guidance |
|------|----------|
| **Default** | SaaS increases **5% annually** — state it on the order form |
| **Required on every deal** | Document the increase method (escalator % **or** fixed year-by-year schedule) in the order form and Pipedrive |
| **Renewals** | Escalators / scheduled prices remain in force unless founder-approved language says otherwise |

### Government customers who resist an explicit escalator

Offer **fixed year-by-year prices** on the order form instead of a percentage escalator language. Economics should still reflect ~**5%** compounding (example shape only — use the deal’s Year-1 price):

```text
Year 1: $20,000
Year 2: $21,000
Year 3: $22,050
```

Do **not** freeze Year-1 pricing across the term without founder approval.

### Discount stacking and termination

| Rule | Guidance |
|------|----------|
| **No automatic stacking** | Multi-year term discount and bundle / multi-product discount do **not** auto-combine |
| **Special termination** | If the customer gets extra termination flexibility, reduce or remove the multi-year / bundle discount |
| **Commitment for term discount** | Three-year ≤5% and five-year ≤8% require real commitment (five-year = **non-cancellable**; three-year = price commitment with annual billing in advance). Soft outs → smaller or zero term discount |

**Acquire authority:** default 3-year + 5% escalator (or equivalent fixed schedule) + ≤5% three-year / ≤8% five-year non-cancellable term discount → Acquire alone if final SaaS still meets **strategic floor** and discounts do not stack beyond this policy. Deeper discounts, stacking exceptions, waived escalator / frozen price, or special termination that keeps full discount → founder / deal desk.

Proposal validity: **90 days** (see [Prepare proposal](../sops/acquire/prepare-proposal.md)).

## Rules

1. Quote at **list price**; negotiate in the **normal close range**; never go below **strategic floor** without founder / deal desk. Do **not** rewrite the price card for market share — use the [Market-share growth program](#market-share-growth-program).  
2. For LE single-module: **RMS 70%** / **CAD 70–75%** of full-stack **list**; ordinary mins **$6,000** / **$7,500**; second module **upgrades to current combined package**.  
3. For Court: citations/month bands (under-50 list **$7,500**); **$5,000** only as founder strategic floor for extremely light courts. For Jail: bed bands (11–20 list **$15,000**); booking uplifts +10–20% when applicable; interfaces/SLA priced separately.  
4. **Custom product work is not a discount lever** — see [Acquire authority](acquire-authority.md) and GTM disqualifiers.  
5. **Implementation / migration / training / integrations:** quote from the respective tables. Included training = remote launch + admin + recorded materials + a **defined** session count. Extra training and all integrations are separate line items. Discounts use **full fee / incentive / net** — never only $0.  
6. **Court / Jail packaging** must match [Go-to-market](../strategy/go-to-market.md).  
7. State **term**, **annual billing in advance**, **5% escalator** (or fixed year-by-year schedule), and any **term discount** — never leave renewal pricing silent; do **not** auto-stack multi-year + bundle discounts.  
8. Record **all concessions explicitly** (full undiscounted value + reductions) — see [Concessions](#concessions-must-be-explicit).  
9. Document pricing math in **Pipedrive** (size inputs, list / normal close / strategic floor, modules, term, escalator, final amount).

## Proposal checklist (pricing)

Before sending a proposal:

- [ ] Sworn officer count noted (if LE in scope)  
- [ ] LE: band + package rule applied (full stack **100%** / RMS **70%** / CAD **70–75%** of list); final ≥ ordinary min or band **strategic floor** (or founder approval for down to $5,000)  
- [ ] If expanding to second core module: pricing **resets to current full-stack** package (or founder exception logged)  
- [ ] Court **citations/month** band chosen if Court in scope (not defaulting to $5,000)  
- [ ] Jail **rated beds** + booking-turnover judgment noted if Jail in scope  
- [ ] Modules match discovery notes  
- [ ] Court final in band **normal close range** (or founder for **$5,000** / custom)  
- [ ] Jail: quote from band **list** (11–20 → **$15,000** list / ~**$12,000** close); uplifts / separate interface & SLA lines documented
