# Two-week advocate plan

**Document type:** Template  
**Status:** v0.1  
**Audience:** Internal — Advocate  
**Related:** [Capture referral](../sops/advocate/capture-referral.md) · [Referral ask](referral-ask.md) · [Advocate CVE](../customer-value-engine/advocate/README.md) · [Hub Advocate Desk](../backlog/design/hub-advocate-desk.md) · [Epic plan goals](../strategy/epic-kpis.md#advocate)

Copy the **blank template** into Hub (Advocate Desk or Home → Plans → Advocate) for a specific period. Do not store completed personal plans in GitBook unless leadership wants a historical example retained below.

**What good looks like:** real referral asks logged on customer orgs, new Pipedrive leads with origin Referral, downstream discovery/demo movement, and a living reference roster.

**System of record:** Pipedrive (origin + next activity) and customer org notes for asks; Hub plan is the Advocate owner’s two-week coaching artifact; executable day work also lives on Hub notecards.

## Example (illustrative)

*Example only — replace dates and names for the real period.*

### Advocate owner — referral + reference plan (2 weeks)

**Period:** Mon Jul 27 – Fri Aug 7, 2026  
**Focus:** Three referral asks · refresh two reference champions  
**SoR:** Pipedrive  

#### Goals (end of 2 weeks)

| Goal | Target |
|------|--------|
| Referral asks made | **3** |
| Referrals captured | **2** |
| Referral → discovery / demo | **1** |
| Reference champions confirmed or refreshed | **2** |

#### Week 1 — Asks

| Day | Block | Work |
|-----|-------|------|
| Mon | 45 min | Pick three healthy customers; schedule asks. |
| Wed | 60 min | Complete referral asks; log on orgs. |
| Fri | 30 min | Scorecard actuals. |

#### Week 2 — Capture + references

| Day | Block | Work |
|-----|-------|------|
| Tue | 45 min | Follow referrals into Pipedrive; set origin + next activity. |
