# Customer release email

**Document type:** Template  
**Status:** v0.1  
**Audience:** Internal — product / Support senders  
**Related:** [Publish product update](../sops/operate/publish-product-update.md)

Use plain language. Do **not** paste raw Azure DevOps release notes. One email = one clear story (or a short monthly digest).

---

## When to send (vs in-app only)

| Send email | In-app “New features” only |
|------------|----------------------------|
| Security / CJIS-relevant change | Tiny bugfix |
| Customer must change a habit or setting | Minor UI polish |
| Feature customers have been asking for | Internal/refactor with no user-visible change |
| Breaking change or temporary workaround | — |
| Multi-module “what’s new this month” digest | — |

---

## Subject lines (pick one pattern)

- Thin Line update: [short benefit]  
- Action needed: [what to do] — Thin Line  
- What’s new in Thin Line — [Month Year]  

---

## Body — feature / improvement

> Hi [Agency / Chief / team],  
>  
> **What’s new:** [One sentence — user benefit, not ticket ID.]  
>  
> **Why it matters:** [Ops outcome — saves time, fewer errors, clearer workflow.]  
>  
> **What to do:** [Nothing / try X / update a setting / train Y role.]  
>  
> **Where to look:** In the app: Help → New features (and/or path to the screen).  
>  
> Questions? Email or Quo us as usual.  
>  
> — Thin Line Software

---

## Body — action required

> Hi [Agency / team],  
>  
> **Action needed by [date if any]:** [Clear instruction.]  
>  
> **Why:** [Risk or benefit.]  
>  
> **If you need help:** Reply to this email or reach us on Quo — we’re glad to walk through it.  
>  
> — Thin Line Software

---

## Body — monthly digest (optional)

> Hi [Agency / team],  
>  
> Here’s what changed in Thin Line over the last few weeks that may affect day-to-day work:  
>  
> 1. **[Theme]** — [one line]  
> 2. **[Theme]** — [one line]  
> 3. **[Theme]** — [one line]  
>  
> Details stay in Help → New features. Reply if you want a short walkthrough.  
>  
> — Thin Line Software
