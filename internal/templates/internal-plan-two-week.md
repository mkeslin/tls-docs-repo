# Two-week internal plan

**Document type:** Template  
**Status:** v0.1  
**Audience:** Internal — company ops  
**Related:** [Hub Internal Desk](../backlog/design/hub-internal-desk.md) · [Epic plan goals](../strategy/epic-kpis.md#internal) · [Hub notecard board](../backlog/design/hub-notecard-board.md)

Copy the **blank template** into Hub (Internal Desk or Home → Plans → Internal) for a specific period. Do not store completed personal plans in GitBook unless leadership wants a historical example retained below.

**What good looks like:** payroll/finance closes on time, CJIS/security items closed with owners, process blockers cleared, and cross-epic dependencies unblocked with a named next action.

**System of record:** Existing payroll, banking, HR, and CJIS tools. Hub plan is the Internal owner’s two-week coaching artifact; executable day work also lives on Hub notecards.
