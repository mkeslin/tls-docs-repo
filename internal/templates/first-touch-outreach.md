# First-touch outreach (LE)

**Document type:** Template  
**Status:** v0.1  
**Audience:** Internal — Acquire  
**Related:** [Generate new leads](../sops/acquire/generate-new-leads.md) · [Go-to-market](../strategy/go-to-market.md)

Short, chief-friendly language. Personalize the bracketed bits. Do not send identical blasts to an entire list in one day.

---

## Email — association / cold (no mutual contact)

**Subject options:**  
- Quick question for [Agency]  
- RMS/CAD for small Texas agencies  
- Following up from the South Plains chiefs list  

**Body:**

> Chief [Last name],  
>  
> I’m Eric Fugate with Thin Line Software — we build RMS and CAD for small Texas agencies (based in Lubbock).  
>  
> I’m reaching out because [Agency] looks like a good fit for the departments we work with best — roughly the size where a full-featured system should still be straightforward to run day to day.  
>  
> Would you be open to a short call (20–30 minutes) to see if it’s worth a deeper look? Many chiefs prefer to poke around our demo site first; happy to send access either way.  
>  
> No hard sell — if timing is wrong, just say so.  
>  
> Eric Fugate  
> Thin Line Software  
> [phone] · [email]

## Field-visit opener

> Chief, Eric Fugate — Thin Line Software. We’re local to Lubbock and work with small agencies on RMS and CAD. I won’t take much of your time — wanted to introduce ourselves and leave a card. If useful, I can send demo access or set a short follow-up call.

---

## After they have demo access (pre-meeting)

> Thanks for looking at the demo. For our call, it helps if you bring the top three workflows that matter most (or anything that felt confusing). I’ll walk those live and answer questions — we keep these conversational rather than a long product tour.

---

## Do / don’t

| Do | Don’t |
|----|-------|
| Name Texas / small-agency focus | Promise custom builds |
| Offer demo access early | Lead with deep discount |
| Keep under ~150 words | Paste the same mail to 50 chiefs the same morning |
| Log theme + next activity in Pipedrive | Track the conversation only in email |
