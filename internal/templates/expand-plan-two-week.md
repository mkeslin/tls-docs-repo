# Two-week expand plan

**Document type:** Template  
**Status:** v0.1  
**Audience:** Internal — Expand  
**Related:** [Expand CVE](../customer-value-engine/expand/README.md) · [Customer success](../customer-value-engine/expand/customer-success.md) · [Expansion](../customer-value-engine/expand/expansion.md) · [Hub Expand Desk](../backlog/design/hub-expand-desk.md) · [Epic plan goals](../strategy/epic-kpis.md#expand)

Copy the **blank template** into Hub (Expand Desk or Home → Plans → Expand) for a specific period. Do not store completed personal plans in GitBook unless leadership wants a historical example retained below.

**What good looks like:** named live accounts with dated next actions, honest at-risk coverage, real expansion conversations (not vibes), and opportunities logged in Pipedrive.

**System of record:** Pipedrive (or equivalent) for expansion opportunities; Hub plan is the Expand owner’s two-week coaching artifact; executable day work also lives on Hub notecards.
