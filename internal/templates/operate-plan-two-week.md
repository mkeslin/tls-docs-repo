# Two-week operate plan

**Document type:** Template  
**Status:** v0.1  
**Audience:** Internal — Operate  
**Related:** [Triage support request](../sops/operate/triage-support-request.md) · [Publish product update](../sops/operate/publish-product-update.md) · [Operate SOPs](../sops/operate/README.md) · [Hub Operate Desk](../backlog/design/hub-operate-desk.md) · [Epic plan goals](../strategy/epic-kpis.md#operate)

Copy the **blank template** into Hub (Operate Desk or Home → Plans → Operate) for a specific period. Do not store completed personal plans in GitBook unless leadership wants a historical example retained below.

**What good looks like:** support requests logged, clear owners on P1/P2, honest first-response samples, intentional ships (not silent deploys), and a Friday check-in with proof from the shared list / board.

**System of record:** Shared support List (or successor) for requests; Hub plan is the Operate owner’s two-week coaching artifact; executable day work also lives on Hub notecards.

## Example (illustrative)

*Example only — replace dates and items for the real period.*

### Operate owner — support + ships plan (2 weeks)

**Period:** Mon Jul 27 – Fri Aug 7, 2026  
**Focus:** Log everything · clear aged P2s · one noted customer-facing ship  
**Support SoR:** Shared support list  

#### Goals (end of 2 weeks)

| Goal | Target |
|------|--------|
| Support requests logged in shared list (%) | **100%** |
| P1 incidents this period | **0** (or named if unavoidable) |
| Time to first response (P2/P3) — sample | **&lt; 1 business day** |
| Reopened issues | **≤ 2** |
| User-visible ships this period | **1** |

#### Week 1 — Triage hygiene

| Day | Block | Work |
|-----|-------|------|
| Mon | 45 min | Inbox → shared list; assign severities; escalate P1s. |
| Daily | 30 min | First-response pass on new P2/P3. |
| Wed | 60 min | Aged P2 review; close or re-own. |
| Fri | 45 min | Scorecard actuals + risk notes. |

#### Week 2 — Ship + stabilize

| Day | Block | Work |
|-----|-------|------|
| Tue | Release window | User-visible ship; watch 48h ticket spike. |
| Thu | 45 min | Reopened / regression check. |
| Fri | 45 min | Check-in + draft next period. |
