## 1. Purpose

Connect **go-live / hypercare** to **ongoing success** with an explicit baton pass.

## 2. Scope

**In scope:** End of default hypercare (~14 days unless engagement says otherwise); transfer of relationship context; first Expand cadence touch.

**Out of scope:** Running hypercare support (Deliver/Fugate per Operate triage); expansion selling (wait until Healthy check-in unless customer asks).

## 3. Owner

**Deliver** prepares; **Expand owner** accepts and owns cadence afterward.

## 4. Trigger

Hypercare window ending (or early exit agreed); go-live complete enough for steady-state Support.

## 5. Preconditions

- Go-live occurred (or soft-live agreed)  
- Hypercare issues known (open vs closed)  
- Expand owner available to accept  

## 6. Inputs

| Input | Source |
|-------|--------|
| Modules live | Engagement workspace / Hub |
| Champions / contacts | Deliver notes |
| Open risks | Hypercare log |
| Training status | Deliver |

## 7. Outputs

- Completed [handoff checklist](../../checklists/deliver-to-expand-handoff-checklist.md)  
- Pipedrive activity: “Expand handoff accepted” + first check-in date  
- Support aware: new tickets → steady-state Support  
- Account enters **new account** cadence (30 / 60 / 90)  

## 8. Tools

| Tool | Use |
|------|-----|
| Engagement workspace | Deliver SoR through hypercare |
| Pipedrive | Org activity + contacts |
| Hub | Expand plan priority for first touches |
| Shared support log | Open items |

## 9. Current state

Transition often informal; Expand may learn about accounts late. Maturity 1.

## 10. Target state

No live account exits hypercare without Expand acceptance and a **Day 30** (or sooner) success touch on the calendar.

## 11. Gap analysis

| Gap | Move toward target |
|-----|--------------------|
| Soft handoff | Mandatory checklist + accept |
| No first touch | Schedule Day 30 at handoff |

## 12. Common risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Open P1 at “exit” | Delay Expand accept or accept as At-risk with 14-day action |
| Wrong contacts | Verify champion before accept |

## 13. Decision trees

```text
Ready to hand off?
    │
    ├─ Blocking P1 open ──► Stay in hypercare / Deliver; do not fake exit
    │
    ├─ Known Watch/At-risk signals ──► Hand off with At-risk band + 14-day action
    │
    └─ Stable enough ──► Expand accepts; schedule Day 30 check-in
```

## 14. Time expectations

| Step | Target |
|------|--------|
| Handoff huddle | At hypercare end (default ~14 days post go-live) |
| First Expand touch | ≤ 30 days after handoff (Day 30 check-in) |

## 16. Procedure

1. Deliver completes [handoff checklist](../../checklists/deliver-to-expand-handoff-checklist.md).  
2. Short huddle: Deliver + Expand owner (+ Support if noisy account).  
3. Expand owner **accepts** (or lists gaps).  
4. Schedule Day 30 success check-in; log Pipedrive activity.  
5. Support treats new tickets as steady-state Operate.  
6. Expand adds account to two-week plan as needed.

## 17. Verification

- [ ] Handoff checklist complete  
- [ ] Expand owner accepted  
- [ ] First check-in scheduled  
- [ ] Pipedrive activity logged  
- [ ] Support aware of steady-state ownership  

## 18. Failure and escalation

| Situation | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| Expand owner unavailable | Founder assigns temporary Expand coverage; do not skip |
| Customer still in heavy training | Extend hypercare explicitly; re-date handoff |
