## 1. Purpose

Grow ARR from **existing** customers with a repeatable expansion conversation — after success is real, or when the customer asks.

## 2. Scope

**In scope:** Additional modules, sites, or seats for live customers; opportunity hygiene in Pipedrive; handoff into proposal/contract when ready.

**Out of scope:** New logos (Acquire); fixing At-risk accounts before pitching (stabilize via check-in first); custom engineering commitments without product/founder path.

## 3. Owner

**Expand owner**

## 4. Trigger

- Healthy (or Watch-with-clear-path) check-in surfaces a module/site gap  
- Customer **inquiry** (email/Quo/call) about another product or site  
- Deliver/Support tips Expand on a warm expansion signal (still qualify health first)

## 5. Preconditions

- Account is not **At-risk** **or** customer initiated the inquiry (then acknowledge risk and do not oversell)  
- Know contracted modules vs wishlist  
- Pricing guardrails available  

## 6. Inputs

| Input | Source |
|-------|--------|
| Health band | Last check-in |
| Modules live vs available | Product / price list / Hub |
| Inquiry details | Customer message |
| Prior notes | Pipedrive |

## 7. Outputs

- Pipedrive **opportunity** (org linked): title, module/site, stage, next step, expected close (even if rough)  
- Activity note from the conversation  
- Next commercial action (demo, quote, follow-up)

## 9. Current state

Opportunity-driven / reactive; uneven Pipedrive hygiene. Maturity 1.

## 10. Target state

Every serious expansion interest becomes a Pipedrive opportunity within **one business day**. Conversations use a short qualification checklist. Pricing stays inside guardrails; exceptions go to founders.

## 11. Gap analysis

| Gap | Move toward target |
|-----|--------------------|
| Vibes, no opportunity | Same-day Pipedrive opportunity |
| Pitching while unhealthy | Health gate (except customer-initiated) |
| Vague next steps | Dated next activity always |

## 12. Common risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Discount spiral | [Pricing guardrails](../../policies/saas-pricing-and-discount-guardrails.md) |
| Promising unbuilt features | Product confirms roadmap; no fake dates |
| Ignoring open fires | Stabilize At-risk first |

## 13. Decision trees

```text
May we open an expansion conversation?
    │
    ├─ Customer inquiry ──► Yes (note health; do not ignore open P1)
    │
    ├─ Healthy check-in with module/site gap ──► Yes
    │
    ├─ Watch ──► Only if gap is clear and no active red-flag crisis
    │
    └─ At-risk ──► No Expand pitch; recover first (unless customer insists — then light listen + reschedule)
```

```text
After conversation?
    │
    ├─ Real interest ──► Create/update Pipedrive opportunity + next step
    │
    ├─ Not now ──► Activity note; return to success cadence
    │
    └─ Needs quote / exception ──► Expand drafts; Acquire/founder per authority
```

## 16. Procedure

1. **Qualify** — Confirm trigger (check-in vs inquiry) and health gate.  
2. **Prepare** — Modules available, rough value, known objections, [Expansion conversation checklist](../../checklists/expansion-conversation-checklist.md).  
3. **Converse** — Problem → fit → interest → next step. No invented ship dates.  
4. **Log** — Pipedrive opportunity + activity.  
5. **Advance** — Demo, quote, or nurture; update Expand plan priorities.  
6. **Close path** — When ready, follow Acquire contract/proposal norms for existing-customer expansion.

## 17. Verification

- [ ] Health gate respected (or inquiry exception noted)  
- [ ] Pipedrive opportunity exists with next step + date  
- [ ] Activity note captured  
- [ ] Pricing path known or escalated  

## 18. Failure and escalation

| Situation | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| Customer wants custom build | Product/founder; do not commit in Expand alone |
| Stalled opportunity > 30 days | Re-qualify or mark lost/nurture with reason |
